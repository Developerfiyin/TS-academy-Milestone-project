How Planetary Data Helps Us Understand Space

Planetary science gives us important information about planets, moons, asteroids, and other objects in space. By studying this data, scientists can better understand our solar system.

Visualizing the Differences Between Planets

Each planet in our solar system has unique characteristics. These differences help scientists understand how planets formed and how they evolved.
